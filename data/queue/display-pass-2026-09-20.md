# Display pass, 2026-09-20

Phase P, row P3: a pass over every published hit (288 on 2026-09-20, main f87eea8) proposing a display where the words read better with a listed form, capitals for I, names and acronyms, or the punctuation the site allows (decision D27). **Nothing here is applied.** Each line is a proposal for the operator; a display reaches the site only through `pnpm hits:display` on a hit the operator names, and the merge of that change.

What was looked for, in every published hit: the word `i` (seven hits); a word whose letters are a listed form the pin lacks (none yet: the forms joined the dictionary the same day, and no published hit was enumerated with them); a word its sense or justification reads as a name, an initialism or a title (Musk, Brits, Quakers, Morgan, Anna, Varna, the Old West, Joe's, Dolly, Trump, Rick, Torchwood, Shiva, Frank, Ted, the Senate, Zion, Jane Smart, Sally, Carl Kook, Yanks, Mad Max, AI); and a reading the justification gives that a comma or a full stop makes plain. A capital at the start of a sentence and a full stop are proposed only where the hit reads as a sentence with I; the pass does not put every display in sentence case, which is a style decision for the operator to make once for all.

Every proposal below passes the display rule against the hit's words in their order. One, `van Joe's kin`, is a possessive, which only the command may set; the judge would have it refused. Words the pass left alone, on purpose: `its` on Doritos, Einstein, Eastwood and the countryside (the possessive reading is the anagram's), `tsar`, `git`, `navy`, `stateside`, `nana`, `kan` and `jin`.
